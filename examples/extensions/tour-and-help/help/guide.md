# The tour and help sample

This article comes with the sample extension **example.tour-and-help**. An extension can add articles like this one to the Extensions group of the help page: the help search finds them, and the AI assistant can read them too.

## What the sample adds

- A page in the left column, drawn by the extension's app script.
- A card in **Settings > Extensions** that decides whether the page shows a tip.
- A guided tour that walks through the extension's card, its page and its card in Settings.

## Play the tour again

1. **From this article**: [Play the tour](action:tour:ext/example.tour-and-help/intro).
2. **From the Extensions page**: open **Details** on the sample's card and press the button of its tour.

> [!TIP]
> A tour you finished or skipped does not play by itself again. A step whose target is not there, such as a page not drawn yet, is skipped.
